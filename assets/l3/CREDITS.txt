Level 3 "Tsunami" assets
All textures are CC0 (public domain) from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com):
  aerial_beach_01, coast_sand_01, coast_land_rocks_01, brown_planks_03, palm_bark
  (+ aerial_grass_rock, shared with Level 2)
All geometry (palms, sharks, swimmers, rescue boat, debris, wave) is procedural; all Level 3 audio
(surf, siren, wave roar, sharks/tension, horn, birds) is synthesized at runtime with Web Audio.

Level 3 watercraft (craft/yacht.glb, craft/jet-ski.glb)
  "Motor Yacht" and "Jet Ski" from the Grab3D Cozy Boats & Aircraft Kit, https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/yacht/ and
  https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/jet-ski/ ; licence CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required).
  Re-encoded for mobile: textures resized to 1024 px WebP, geometry meshopt-compressed (gltf-transform). Jet-ski hood re-tinted per rider in-shader.
meshopt_decoder.module.js (vendor/addons/libs) - meshoptimizer by Arseny Kapoulkine, MIT licence (from three.js r160).
