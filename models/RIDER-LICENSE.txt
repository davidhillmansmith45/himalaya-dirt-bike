models/rider.glb
Base body + 53-bone rig: "Human base mesh with editable 53-bone rig" by Innerscene,
built from MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 assets, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/human-base-mesh-with-editable-53-bone-rig-8e7c8ab1
Modifications: welded + simplified to ~11k triangles (gltf-transform). MX gear (jersey, pants,
boots, gloves), helmet, goggles and neck brace are generated procedurally in rider.js.
